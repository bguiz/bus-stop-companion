# Bus Stop Companion: demo video script

- **Length:** about 52 s (hard limit 60 s)
- **Format:** MP4, 1920×1080 (Full HD), 30 fps
- **Audio:** a quiet, minimal music bed (soft lo-fi, about 85–90 BPM) plus a few sound effects on real on-screen actions. No voiceover.
- **Bottom text:** the lines a narrator would say, shown as subtitles, one phrase at a time.
- **Right side:** a Japanese title in vertical, top-to-bottom writing (縦書き) while each feature is shown. It reads like an anime episode title and runs down from the top of the frame.
- **Footage:** the real app, recorded live at the stop "Opp Orchard Stn Exit 12" (13191) in a fresh browser profile at phone size (430×932, 2× pixel density). Live data (bus times, weather, GPS, taxis) is whatever the APIs return at recording time, so the exact numbers and characters on screen may differ from the examples below. Character art comes from AniList, as the app shows it.

## Look

- The app's own colours: navy ground (`#2b4a7a` → `#0f1830`), amber accent `#ffb454`.
- The phone screen sits left of centre. For key moments, the part being used (a bus card, a tag, the radar) lifts out of the phone at about 1.7× size, then drops back into place.
- Subtitles: Inter Bold, white, centred near the bottom.
- Japanese titles: Noto Sans JP Black, amber, large, in one or two vertical columns on the right. Characters appear one by one from the top down.

## Notes

- The hook's grey rows are a plain list made for the video, not a screenshot of any real app, and no other app is named.
- Every claim in the subtitles comes from the app and its PRD: search by name, road or code; live minutes, crowd load and bus type; lines that react to weather and crowd; boarding adds to the collection; scene with real GPS and taxi spirits; a 136-character pool; the same service number always maps to the same character.
- The recording uses its own browser profile, so it does not touch your real collection or open-day log.
